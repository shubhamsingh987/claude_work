How it works

No app, no account, no pairing menu. This is the entire setup process.

  1. 1. Power on both units

    Each one names itself automatically and runs a quick self-test on its mic and speaker — you'll see it come alive in a couple seconds.

  2. 2. They find each other

    Each radio broadcasts a beacon and listens for others on the same channel. Two freshly powered-on units typically discover each other within a few seconds — no button press needed.

  3. 3. Hold the button, talk

    Press and hold to speak — your voice goes out live to every unit in range at once. Let go, and you're back to listening. Classic hold-to-talk, group style.

Built for "just works," not "just launched"

A small, honest feature set — everything here is something the firmware actually does.

Auto-discovery, always

Any unit that comes into range is added to the mesh automatically. Walk away, come back — it reconnects on its own, no re-pairing.

Party-line, not 1:1 calling

There's no dialing a specific unit. Press the button and everyone powered-on in range hears you — built for groups, not pairs.

No SIM, no internet, no fees

Radios talk directly to each other over 2.4GHz. No router, no data plan, no account, and nothing stops working because your phone has no bars.

Long-range radio mode

Runs on Espressif's long-range PHY by default — extra reach and a more robust link versus standard WiFi-band radio, on the same hardware.

Runs on a power bank

Draws a few hundred milliamps. A basic USB power bank comfortably gets you through a day of hiking, an event, or a shift.

Optional status screen

Add a small OLED and see your unit's name, battery estimate, and every nearby peer's signal strength at a glance.

About that 100m

In open air with a clear line of sight, PiWalkTalk units comfortably reach around 100 metres. That's the honest number, and it moves depending on your surroundings — the same as any handheld radio.

  • Open field, line of sight — best case, easily 100m+.
  • Indoors, through walls — expect noticeably less, same as WiFi.
  • Dense obstacles / interference — range drops further, as with any 2.4GHz radio.

Questions

Do I need the internet or a SIM card?

No. The two units talk directly to each other over 2.4GHz radio. No router, no WiFi network, no SIM, no data plan.

How many people can join in?

More than two. Any powered-on unit within range joins the mesh automatically and hears whoever is talking — it's built as a group intercom, not a 1:1 call.

Can two people talk at the same time?

It's half-duplex, like a classic walkie-talkie: whoever keys up first holds the floor until they let go, so audio never gets garbled by two people talking over each other.

Is it private / encrypted?

No — by design, this is an open broadcast with no pairing and no encryption. Anyone with a compatible unit on the same channel can transmit and listen. It's built for casual group use — camping, events, job sites — not for private communications.

What's the actual range?

Around 100 metres in open air with line of sight. Walls, floors, and obstacles reduce that, the same as with WiFi or any handheld radio — see the range section above.
